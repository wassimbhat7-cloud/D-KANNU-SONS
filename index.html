Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common farming questions

What crops do you have seeds for? ▼

We carry seeds for a wide range of crops including cereals (rice, maize), pulses (groundnuts, chickpeas), vegetables (tomato, onion, brinjal), and local varieties preferred by Harur farmers. Our seed selection is updated seasonally based on market demand and local agricultural needs.

How do I choose the right fertilizer for my crop? ▼

The right fertilizer depends on your crop type, soil conditions, and growth stage. Our agronomists can help you select appropriate nutrient solutions after understanding your field requirements. Visit us or fill the advisory form to get personalized recommendations.

What pest management solutions do you recommend? ▼

We offer both organic and chemical pest management solutions including biopesticides, fungicides, and herbicides. Our team provides guidance on integrated pest management (IPM) approaches tailored to your crop and the current pest pressure.

Can I get advisory support if I'm a new farmer? ▼

Absolutely. We welcome new farmers and provide comprehensive guidance from crop planning to harvest. Our advisory clinic specifically helps farmers make informed decisions about products and cultivation practices. First consultation is complimentary.

Do you have organic/certified seeds and fertilizers? ▼

Yes, we stock certified organic seeds and bio-based fertilizers for farmers interested in organic farming. We can guide you through organic certification processes and provide appropriate product recommendations.

What's the best time to visit your store? ▼

We're open year-round during standard business hours. However, the peak seasons are just before monsoon and winter planting periods. Call ahead during these times for faster service. We also offer consultations by appointment.

How can I stay updated on new products and seasonal offers? ▼

Connect with us via WhatsApp or email to receive seasonal product updates and farming tips. We also share advisory content on crop management best practices. Contact us through the form to join our farmer network.

Do you offer bulk discounts for cooperative societies or farmer groups? ▼

Yes, we offer special rates for bulk orders from farmer cooperatives, self-help groups, and agricultural societies. Contact us directly with your group's requirements for customized quotations.
